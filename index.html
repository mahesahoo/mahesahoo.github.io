About

Learning by Building

I'm currently pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science at ITER, SOA, alongside a BS in Data Science & Applications from IIT Madras. It's a lot some weeks — but I'm grateful for the room both give me to go deep in software while staying close to data and systems.

Most of what I actually build sits between AI engineering and the small, unglamorous problems people run into day to day — a productivity tracker, a teaching assistant that answers real questions. I'm also curious about neuroscience and psychology, less as a specialty and more because understanding how minds work, mine included, tends to make the engineering better.

None of this comes from having it figured out. It comes from trying, getting stuck, asking people who know more than I do, and building again. I owe a lot of it to people who answered a question, reviewed a line of code, or were just patient while I learned.
